  1. Open it in Safari

    Tap Open the timer above. Use Safari, the blue compass app, because other apps can't add it to your home screen properly.

  2. Tap Share

    Tap the share button . On newer iPhones it may be inside the ••• button at the bottom right.

  3. Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen. If you see an Open as Web App switch, leave it on.

  4. Tap Add

    It's now on your home screen as Interval and opens full screen like an app. Use it from there from now on.

  1. Open it in Chrome

    Tap Open the timer above. It should open in Chrome (or Samsung Internet).

  2. Open the menu

    Tap the ⋮ button in the top right corner. Chrome may also show an Install banner at the bottom; tapping that works too.

  3. Install

    Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.

  4. Done

    It's now on your home screen as Interval and opens full screen like an app.

Samsung Internet: open the timer, tap ☰ at the bottom, then Add page to → Home screen.

Good to know

🎵
Your music keeps playing. The beeps play over Spotify, Apple Music or whatever you're listening to.
🔔
iPhone: turn off Silent mode or you won't hear the beeps. Your music isn't affected either way.
🔊
Android: beeps use your media volume, the same as your music. Turn it up if you can't hear them.
👥
Training together? One person taps Start group. Everyone else scans their QR code, or taps Join group and types the code on the leader's screen. All the phones start at the same second.
📱
Keep the screen on while you train. If the beeps ever stop, tap the screen once to bring them back.
